## What you're actually buying: native IPs vs. dual-ISP residential IPs

Before getting into the pricing table, it's worth understanding the two product families LisaHost sells, because they solve different problems and are priced differently.

**Native IP VPS** plans (like the US 9929 line, Singapore, Japan, or the German dual-stack IPv4+IPv6 plans) give you an IP address that's registered to the actual country/region rather than a foreign IP mapped through NAT. They're aimed at people who need to appear as a genuine local visitor — for streaming unlocks, regional app stores, or basic geo-restricted access — without needing the deeper "looks like a home internet connection" attribute.

**Dual-ISP residential VPS/VDS** plans go a step further. These IPs carry ISP attribution tied to real consumer broadband providers — AS4837 and 9929 dual-ISP blocks in the US, iCable and HGC in Hong Kong, IIJ in Japan, Sky Telecom in the UK. When a platform like TikTok, Instagram, or a payment gateway checks the IP, it sees residential/ISP classification rather than a hosting-provider ASN. This is the category most social media operators, TikTok shop sellers, and cross-border e-commerce accounts are actually shopping for when they land on LisaHost's pricing page, and it's priced noticeably higher than the plain native-IP tier for the same specs.

There's also a smaller category of **static residential IP VDS** — physical broadband connections in real US homes (Astound Broadband in Los Angeles, Atlas Networks in Seattle) rather than virtualized native IP blocks. These are the most "authentic" option on the site, but they come with a catch worth flagging clearly: unlike the standard 48-hour unconditional refund that applies to most VPS plans, these static residential VDS products are marked on the official order page as **"特殊产品，仅退网站余额"** — meaning any refund goes back to your account balance rather than your original payment method. If flexibility on refunds matters to you, that's a detail to weigh before ordering one of those specifically.
